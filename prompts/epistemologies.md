# Prompt: Epistemic Cultures University Cutaway

## Style & Tone
* Bright, colourful, high-saturation cartoon illustration
* Clean linework, soft shading, slightly storybook / educational poster style
* Friendly, non-threatening, optimistic tone
* All characters appear content and engaged in their work
* No text labels anywhere in the image
* Slightly idealised “campus brochure” aesthetic
* Perspective: straight-on cross-sectional “cutaway dollhouse” view

## Overall Composition
* A symmetrical, classical university building shown in cutaway cross-section
* Two main floors plus an attic/roof section
* Exterior: light stone, arched doorway, slate roof, trees and blue sky outside
* Interior: rooms clearly visible like a dollhouse, each representing a different epistemic approach
* Warm interior lighting, consistent across rooms

## Rooms and Their Contents

🧪 Ground Floor Left — Randomised Trial
* A small clinical/research room
* A queue of people waiting in line
* A researcher handing out pills from a bottle marked only with a “?” symbol
* The participants look neutral/curious, not distressed
* Suggests controlled experimentation without text explanation

## Ground Floor Right — Scientific Services / Instrumentation
* A high-tech lab environment
* Large scientific instruments (microscope, analytical machines, screens with data visualisations)
* A small queue of researchers waiting to use equipment
* One central operator:
* Curly hair
* Wearing a green jumper
* Seated at a computer controlling instruments
* This person represents a service-oriented research role

## First Floor Left — Mathematics / Theory
* A mathematician at a blackboard
* Covered in equations, diagrams, geometric sketches
* Actively writing or explaining
* Room is simple, focused, intellectual

# First Floor Centre — Humanities
* A cosy library/study
* Bookshelves filled with books
* A scholar sitting in a comfortable chair reading a book
* Warm lamp lighting, calm atmosphere

## First Floor Right — Engineering / “What Works”
* A workshop or maker space
* Tools on walls, mechanical parts on a bench
* An engineer tinkering with a device
* Mid-construction machinery or prototype
* Emphasis on hands-on experimentation

## Roof / Attic — Observational Science
* An astronomer using a telescope
* Telescope protrudes out through an opening in the roof toward the sky
* Night sky visible behind them (stars, galaxies)
* The astronomer is a Black character
* Calm, observational posture

## Diversity & People
* Include visible diversity across characters:
* At least one Black academic (astronomer)
* At least one Asian academic (e.g. humanities scholar or engineer)
* Mixed genders and ages
* Everyone appears competent and engaged

## Visual Goals
* Each room should visually communicate a different way of producing knowledge
* No hierarchy implied — all rooms equally detailed and appealing
* Balanced composition so no room dominates
* The viewer should be able to “read” the differences without labels

## Technical Suggestions (optional depending on system)
* Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 3:2 (wide landscape)
* Medium-high detail
* Sharp focus across entire image (no depth blur)
* Lighting: warm indoors, bright daylight outdoors
* Avoid clutter but include enough detail for storytelling

## Negative Prompt (if supported)
* No text, labels, captions, or speech bubbles
* No dark, dystopian, or distressing imagery
* No photorealism (keep it illustrated/cartoon)
* No exaggerated caricatures or stereotypes
