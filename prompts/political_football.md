# Core Prompt

A vivid, high-saturation cartoon / comic-style illustration of a football (soccer) penalty scene at night.

Camera framing: low angle from behind and slightly to the left of the striker, showing only the lower leg and foot (no upper body). The leg is in the left foreground, angled toward the right. The goal is centred in the distance, empty, with a clear view of the net.

Foreground subject: a football on the penalty spot is replaced by a stylised atomic model — a bright nucleus (red and blue spheres clustered together) with thin elliptical electron orbits and small blue electrons circling. It should read clearly as a “science atom icon”, not realistic physics.

The player is about to kick: the boot is planted just behind the atom, slightly lifted heel, suggesting imminent motion.

Clothing: the player wears a dark navy football sock. On the sock are clean, graphic logos resembling UK political party branding, arranged vertically. Include:
* a red rose-style emblem (Labour-like)
* a yellow bird emblem (Liberal Democrat-like)
* a blue circular arrow-style emblem labelled “REFORM”
* a simple white scribble-style emblem near the top (Conservative-like)

The logos should look like flat vector graphics printed on fabric, not photorealistic badges.

## Environment: 

a large football stadium at night under bright floodlights, with glowing lights visible at the top right. The crowd is suggested with soft, blurred dots of colour, not detailed faces.

## Pitch: 

vibrant green grass with a clearly marked white penalty spot and line.

## Goal: 

white frame and net, cleanly rendered, centred, slightly blurred by depth of field but still sharp enough to read.

## Lighting: 

strong stadium lighting with cool blue night tones, bright highlights on the grass and boot, and subtle shadows under the atom and foot.

## Style: 

bold outlines, smooth gradients, slightly exaggerated proportions, high colour contrast, polished digital illustration, similar to modern sports graphics or editorial cartoon art.

## Mood: 

dynamic and anticipatory — just before the kick. No motion blur yet, but tension implied.

# Negative Prompt (important for consistency)

No full body, no face, no multiple players, no goalkeeper, no daytime lighting, no realistic soccer ball, no text outside logos, no messy or photorealistic textures, no political slogans, no crowd detail, no camera distortion.

# Optional Parameters (if supported)

* Aspect ratio: 1:1 or 4:3
* Style strength: medium-high (to keep it cartoon but controlled)
* Detail level: high
* Camera: 35–50mm equivalent, shallow depth of field
* Sampling: prioritise sharp edges and colour clarity over painterly effects
