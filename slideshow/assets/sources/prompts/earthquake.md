# Core prompt

A high-colour, cartoon-style isometric (bird’s-eye) illustration of a modern city immediately after an earthquake.

The scene is split naturally (not with a line) into two contrasting areas:

Upper / central area (well-engineered buildings):

* Mid-rise and high-rise buildings are intact, upright, and undamaged
* *Clean vertical lines, symmetrical shapes, glass and concrete facades
* *Streets are orderly, cars moving normally, small park or plaza with trees and a fountain
* Subtle visual cues of strong engineering (e.g. visible structural frames, regular window grids, slight hints of reinforced design)
* Bright, calm atmosphere

Lower / foreground area (poorly constructed buildings):
* Several buildings have partially or fully collapsed (“pancaked” floors, broken walls, rubble piles)
* Irregular shapes, tilted structures, exposed interiors
* Debris scattered across streets, damaged cars, disrupted roads
* No graphic injury or distress; keep it sanitised and non-triggering

Overall composition:
* Smooth visual transition between the two zones (no hard boundary)
* *Same urban grid layout continues across both areas to emphasise that only construction quality differs
* Isometric perspective (about 30–45 degree angle), wide view of multiple city blocks
* Bright, saturated colours, clean outlines, slightly simplified/cartoonish but still detailed
* Educational / infographic feel without any text, labels, arrows, or icons

Mood and purpose:
* Visually communicate that better engineering and building codes lead to safer, more resilient buildings
* Clear contrast but not exaggerated to the point of absurdity

High detail, crisp rendering, consistent lighting, no text anywhere in the image.

# Negative prompt (important for consistency)

No text, labels, arrows, checkmarks, or infographic boxes.
No photorealism.
No dark, gritty, or traumatic imagery.
No fire, smoke plumes, or emergency scenes dominating the image.
No exaggerated destruction that looks like war.
No camera perspective at street level (must be bird’s-eye / isometric).
No fantasy or sci-fi elements.

# Optional control knobs

* Style keywords: “isometric illustration, vector-like shading, soft shadows, architectural diagram style, clean line art”
* Composition hints (if supported): “rule of thirds, intact buildings in upper half, damaged buildings in lower third”
* Lighting: “daytime, even lighting, minimal shadows, clear visibility”
